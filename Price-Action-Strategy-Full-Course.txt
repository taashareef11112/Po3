What is Price ?
  - Depend upon supply and demand.
  - If Demand More, --- Price Increase (Buyer Start
    Buying) [ Support Level]
  - If Oversupply - Price Falls(More Seller)
    [ Resistance Level]


   Market Going Up Means - Demand
   Market Going Down Means - Supply
